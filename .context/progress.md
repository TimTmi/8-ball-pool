# Progress: 8 Ball Pool

## Current State
- Unity Project folder structure has been set up.
- UI Toolkit assets (UXML/USS) for Main Menu, Gameplay, and Credits created.
- Basic UI Controllers implemented.
- Main Menu and Gameplay scenes instantiated and added to Build Settings.
- Phase 2 Core Gameplay Mechanics implemented: InputManager captures drag input, GameplayUIController exposes lock toggles, shoot button, and spin control.
- **Phase 3 (Partial): Visual & Physics Setup** - Procedural generation of 2D pool sprites (felt, cushions, pockets, 16 balls, cue stick) added via Editor script. `TableSetup` dynamically builds the physics and visual layout at runtime using authoritative constants from `TableLayout`.
- **Phase 3 (Partial): Shot Physics & Cushion Bounce** - `CueController` (on the Table object) launches the cue ball from aim angle + power, and reports when every ball has come to rest. `Ball` owns each ball's motion state and snaps near-zero velocity to a full stop. `Cushion` drains the surplus rebound energy on rail hits. Aiming and the cue stick are suppressed while the shot plays out.
- Collider sizing in `TableSetup` was corrected: ball, pocket, and rail colliders are now authored in local space, so they match their sprites instead of being scaled to the wrong size by the transform.
- **Phase 4 (Partial): Pocketing** - The rails are now six runs with a mouth at every pocket, so balls can physically enter one. `Pocket` captures a ball when its centre reaches the hole; `Ball.Drop()` takes it out of play with a short sink. A scratched cue ball is returned to the head spot once the table settles.
- Aim/power controls rewritten (2026-08-31, same-side scheme): aim angle = direction from pointer through the cue ball (finger drags on the cue's side, pull-back style), power = pointer-to-cue-ball distance (full power at `TableLayout.HalfFeltWidth`, tunable via `_maxPowerDistance`). Replaces the old drag-delta scheme.
- Pull distance made 1:1 (2026-08-31): pointer-to-cue-ball distance now maps directly to the cue stick pull-back, capped at `_maxPullDistance` (default 2.5u) in `InputManager`, which also defines full power. The cue visual reads the same field, replacing its own hardcoded 2.5u pull.
- Cue sprite orientation fixed (2026-08-31): cue art now has the tip (chalk) on the right (+X), matching the runtime assumption that the sprite's +X axis points at the cue ball (`InputManager.UpdateCueVisuals`). Previously the butt faced the ball.
- HUD press isolation fixed (2026-08-31): `GameplayUIController.IsPointerPressOnUI` marks presses that belong to the HUD (shoot button, lock toggles, spin button/panel, or the tap that closes the spin panel); `InputManager` ignores them for aim/power. Replaces the narrower `IsSpinInteracting` flag, which is deleted.
- Locks cleared on turn end (2026-09-01): `GameplayUIController.UnlockAimAndPower()` unticks both lock toggles, called from `InputManager.HandleShoot` after a successful shot, so each new turn starts with aim and power unlocked.
- Drag cancel zone (2026-09-01): `InputManager` snapshots aim/power/has-aim when a drag starts; while the pointer stays within `_cancelRadius` (serialized, default 0.6u) of the cue ball, aim and power revert to the snapshot, and releasing inside the zone cancels the drag (no shoot button). Dragging back out of the zone resumes aiming.
- Power bar (2026-09-01): while dragging with power unlocked, a world-space bar (`PowerBar` component, spawned as a Table child by `TableSetup.SetupPowerBar`) floats below the cue ball and fills 0→1 with `CurrentPower`. `InputManager.UpdatePowerBar` owns visibility (dragging + not power-locked + table settled) and hides it otherwise.
- Rail look reworked (2026-09-01): brighter wood rail (dark runtime tint removed from `TableSetup.SpawnRail`) plus a darker-green cushion pad strip (`RailCushion` sprite, `TableLayout.CushionPadThickness`) spawned on the felt-facing edge of each rail run by `TableSetup.SpawnCushionPad`; visual only, collider unchanged.
- Closed rail frame (2026-09-01): rail wood now runs past every pocket so the six segments join into a complete rectangular frame with the pocket holes cut into it. `TableLayout.RailSegment` gained `VisualCenter`/`VisualSize` (full wooden run) alongside `Center`/`Size` (cushion-only span); `TableSetup.SpawnRail` scales the sprite to the visual run and offsets/shrinks the BoxCollider2D to the cushion span, so pocket mouths stay physically open.
- Rounded table corners (2026-09-01): `SpriteGenerator.GenerateRailCorner` produces a wood corner cap rounded at its outer corner (0.25u arc, highlight rim); `TableSetup.SetupRailCorners`/`SpawnRailCorner` place four rotated caps over the square rail-run corners (sortingOrder -7). Visual only, colliders unchanged. Pocket sprites raised to sortingOrder -6 so the holes draw above the caps. Rail visual runs were shortened to stop at the felt edges (`TableLayout` longVisualLength = HalfFeltWidth, side height = FeltHeight) so the wood doesn't show through the caps' rounded notches; the caps alone close the corners.
- Real-life proportions (2026-09-01): ball shrunk 0.5u → 0.2u (4.5% of felt width, like a 2.25" ball on a 9-ft table); `PocketRadius` and `PocketMouthHalfWidth` now derive from `BallDiameter` (0.8× / 0.95×), so the mouth stays 1.9× ball and the hole 1.6× ball at any size. Felt (9×4.5, 2:1), rail thickness 0.4u (~9% of playing width), and spots unchanged — they already matched. `SetupPhysicsPrefabs` editor script now reads `TableLayout` instead of hardcoded sizes. Not yet compiled/run (no Unity CLI available); ball-relative values that may need retune: `CueController` shot speeds (u/s now spans ~130 ball-diameters/s at max), `InputManager._cancelRadius` (0.6u = 3 ball diameters), `Ball.StopSpeedThreshold` (0.06 u/s).
- Turn system (2026-09-01): `TurnManager` (on the Table object in `Gameplay.unity`) subscribes to `CueController.OnTableSettled` and unconditionally hands the turn to the other player (`CurrentPlayerIndex` 0/1, event `OnTurnStarted`). `Ball.IsMoving` now also counts angular velocity (threshold `StopAngularSpeedThreshold` 0.5 rad/s), and `CueController` calls `Ball.Stabilize()` on every ball before raising settle, so each turn starts from a fully stopped table. `InputManager` relays the event to `GameplayUIController.SetTurnLabelText`, updating the top-bar `player-turn-label`. No rules logic — fouls, groups, and win/loss still pending. Not yet compiled/run (no Unity CLI).
- HUD hidden during shot (2026-09-01): `GameplayUIController.SetInputHudVisible(bool)` toggles the bottom bar (locks + shoot) and spin button (`bottom-bar` gained a UXML name for lookup), closing the spin panel on hide; the turn label stays. `InputManager` hides it after a successful shot and shows it again in `HandleTurnStarted`. First turn starts visible (UXML default).

- Aim line (2026-09-01): `ShotPrediction` (one `Physics2D.CircleCast` of the cue ball shape along the aim direction) plus `AimLine` (pooled sprite dots on the Table object, created by `TableSetup.SetupAimLine`) draw a dotted path to first contact, a ghost ball where the cue ball lands at contact, and a short line for the struck ball's departure. `InputManager.UpdateAimLine` shows it on the same condition as the cue stick (`CanAim && _hasAim`), so it clears during the stroke and the shot. Straight-line only: no spin, no cue-ball path after contact, no rail bounce. Dots and ghost carry a thin baked-in outline (`_outlineWidth`, 0.006u — under a screen pixel at ortho 4) so they read against the felt. Compiles clean; not run.

- Spin (2026-09-01): `SpinModel` holds the formulas, shared by the live shot and the preview. Top/back spin fires on the first ball struck (`CueBallSpin`), side spin curves the roll and swings the rebound off a rail (`Cushion` owns that so the two callbacks cannot race). Direction follows the 8 Ball Pool guide: left spin moves the ball left. Measured: 5.3 degrees of curve at full side spin, power-independent; ±19.3 degrees of cushion swing; back spin reverses a full hit at 0.45x impact speed. `ShotPrediction` became a stepped walk at `Time.fixedDeltaTime` (a coarser clock drew the line 0.22u long), and `AimLine` now draws the post-contact cue path in a third colour. Compiles clean; not run.

## Blockers / Notes
- Spin (`GameplayUIController.CurrentSpin`) is captured by the UI but not yet consumed by the shot; a top-down 2D cue ball needs an explicit curve/english model, planned with the trajectory work.
- Pocketing is mechanical only: no foul detection, no turn switching, no win/loss, and a returned cue ball is not checked against balls already sitting on the head spot.
